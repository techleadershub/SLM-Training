# Nova Home Appliances: user manual (fictional)

## Installation: levelling and transit bolts
Before the first use, remove the 4 transit bolts at the back of the NovaWash WM-7. Turn the 4 feet until the machine does not rock, and check it with a spirit level. Leave 5 cm of space behind the machine.

## Cycle guide: Cotton
The Cotton cycle at 60 °C takes 2 hours 20 minutes. The maximum load is 7 kg.

## Cycle guide: Quick wash
The Quick cycle takes 15 minutes at 30 °C. The maximum load is 2 kg.

## Cycle guide: Delicates and wool
Delicates and wool are washed cold, at up to 30 °C, with a spin speed of at most 600 rpm. The maximum load is 3 kg.

## Detergent
Use only high-efficiency (HE) detergent. One scoop, about 40 ml, is enough for a normal load. Too much detergent makes the machine rinse again and adds about 20 minutes to the cycle.

## Error E21: drain problem
The machine could not drain the water within 5 minutes. Switch off the machine, open the filter cover at the lower front, place a tray under it, unscrew the filter and clear it. Up to 1 litre of water can come out.

## Error E47: door not locked
The door lock did not engage. Press the door firmly until you hear a click, and check that no sock or strap is trapped in the rubber seal. If the error returns after 3 attempts, call service.

## Error E03: no water inflow
The machine is not getting water. Check that the tap is fully open, clean the mesh filter at the hose inlet, and check that the hose is not kinked.

## Error F03: motor overheating
The motor has overheated. Stop the machine, unplug it and wait 30 minutes before restarting. Do not overload the machine. If the error repeats, call service.

## Error U1: unbalanced load
The load is unevenly spread and the drum shakes. Pause the cycle and spread the clothes out. Do not wash a single heavy item such as a blanket alone; add 2 or 3 towels.

## Child lock
Hold the Start and Temp buttons together for 3 seconds to lock or unlock the control panel.

## Cleaning the drum
Run the Tub Clean cycle once a month at 70 °C with no clothes in the drum.

## AC filter cleaning
In summer, clean the NovaCool AC-5 filter every 2 weeks. Slide the filter out, rinse it in lukewarm water below 40 °C, dry it in the shade and refit it.

## AC modes and temperature range
The AC-5 has Cool, Dry, Fan, Auto and Sleep modes. The set temperature can be from 16 °C to 30 °C. In Sleep mode the set temperature rises by 1 °C after 1 hour and by another 1 °C after 2 hours.

## Error CH01: indoor sensor fault
The indoor temperature sensor is disconnected or faulty. Switch the AC off at the plug for 5 minutes and switch on again. If the error stays, call service.

## Error CH05: communication error
The indoor and the outdoor unit cannot communicate. Check the connecting cable for damage. Call service if the cable looks fine.

## Error CH38: low refrigerant
The refrigerant level is low. Never try to refill it yourself; call an authorised service engineer.

## AC remote control
The remote uses 2 AAA batteries. Replace them when the display fades. Point the remote at the receiver from no more than 8 m away.
