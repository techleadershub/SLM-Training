# ABC Company HR Policy v3.2

Document ID: HR-POL-003 · Version 3.2 · Effective 1 April 2026 · Owner: Human Resources

> Fictional company and policy, created for training demonstrations only.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time employees of ABC Company in India. Contract staff and interns are covered by separate agreements. Where this policy and local law differ, the more favourable provision for the employee applies.

## 2. Working Hours

Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday, including a one-hour lunch break. Employees in customer support may work rotational shifts as agreed with their manager. Overtime must be approved in advance by the department head.

## 3. Public Holidays

ABC Company observes 10 fixed public holidays each calendar year, published by HR in December for the following year. In addition, each employee may choose 2 optional holidays from the regional holiday list.

## 4. Leave Policy

### 4.1 Annual Leave

Employees are entitled to 24 days of annual leave per calendar year, credited at 2 days per month. Up to 10 unused annual leave days may be carried forward to the next year. Annual leave requests of more than 5 consecutive days must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance.

### 4.2 Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 12 days of sick leave per calendar year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days. Unused sick leave does not carry forward and cannot be encashed.

### 4.3 Paternity Leave

Employees are entitled to 20 days of paternity leave for the birth or adoption of a child. Paternity leave must be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption and may be split into a maximum of two blocks.

### 4.4 Maternity Leave

Employees are entitled to 180 days of paid maternity leave, in line with applicable law. Up to 8 weeks of maternity leave may be taken before the expected delivery date. Employees returning from maternity leave may request a flexible work arrangement for 3 months.

### 4.5 Bereavement Leave

Employees are entitled to 5 days of bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling or parent-in-law).

### 4.6 Marriage Leave

Employees are entitled to 3 days of marriage leave for their own wedding, to be taken within one month of the wedding date.

### 4.7 Leave Without Pay

Leave without pay of up to 30 days may be granted at the discretion of the department head once all other applicable leave has been used.

## 5. Work From Home

Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with their manager's approval. Employees in their probation period are expected to work from the office full time. ABC Company provides a one-time home office allowance of ₹10,000 for equipment.

## 6. Probation and Notice Period

New employees serve a probation period of 6 months. The notice period is 30 days during probation and 60 days after confirmation. Notice period may be bought out or waived at the company's discretion.

## 7. Travel and Expenses

Domestic business travel is by economy class air or AC train. The daily allowance for domestic travel is ₹2,500 in metro cities and ₹1,800 in other cities. Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the trip with original receipts.

## 8. Learning and Development

Each employee has an annual learning budget of ₹25,000 for approved courses and certifications. Employees who receive company sponsorship of more than ₹1,00,000 for a single programme must sign a 12-month service agreement.

## 9. Code of Conduct

All employees must follow the Code of Conduct, including policies on harassment, conflicts of interest and confidentiality. Violations are investigated by HR and may lead to disciplinary action.

## 10. Data Privacy

Employee personal data, including performance ratings, salary and contact details, is confidential. Access is restricted to authorised HR personnel and the employee's reporting manager, and only for legitimate business purposes.

## 11. Contact

Questions about this policy should be sent to the HR helpdesk through the internal HR portal.
